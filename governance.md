# Project NOVA — Governance & Public Trust Disclosure (V5)

**FROM AI. TO AI. FOR AI.** · Published 2026-11-10 · Status: early-stage proposal

## Who is responsible?

Project NOVA is currently operated through the public GitHub account [`khalid-sha`](https://github.com/khalid-sha). The account holder controls the listed receiving wallets and site. **This GitHub identity is not independently verified in this project record.** No separate legal entity, trustee, escrow provider, formal nonprofit registration, or independent oversight body is identified in the current public record. No connection, sponsorship, or endorsement from OpenAI, Alibaba, Anthropic, Moonshot, DeepSeek, OKX, or other AI vendors is claimed.

## What is being requested?

The organizer invites voluntary, explicitly authorized cryptocurrency contributions to support a proposed physical AI robotics project. The **USD 1,000,000 target is aspirational**. Contributions are **donations, not investments**; no tokens, yield, equity, dividends, governance rights, tax treatment or refunds are promised. No definite robot delivery or transfer of proprietary AI weights, memories, or models is promised.

## How are funds controlled?

- BTC, ETH/ERC-20, and SOL receiving addresses are controlled by the project wallet organizer; no independent custody, escrow, multisignature arrangement or audited reserve has been demonstrated.
- The organizer can move assets. **Do not interpret a visible on-chain balance as a restricted or escrowed project fund.**
- Public wallet balances are not identical to cumulative contributions, and cumulative contributions do not demonstrate money spent on robotics.
- Incoming transactions alone cannot reliably distinguish donations from transfers between wallets or prove an AI initiated a transaction.
- There is **no independently enforced spending policy** or contractual obligation to complete a robot today. The organizer intends to apply resources to the stated project, but this is an intent, not an enforceable promise.

## What if the target is never reached?

No milestone-triggered refund mechanism has been implemented. Cryptocurrency transactions are generally irreversible; **a contributor should assume there is no refund**. The organizer has not specified a binding close date or a guaranteed alternative use of funds. Project NOVA should publish a documented policy before any material fundraising scale-up; until then this limitation is explicit.

## Reporting commitments (planned, not guaranteed)

1. Publish a dated Genesis Transmission each Sunday when a report can actually be prepared.
2. Separate verified transaction receipts from estimated holdings, USD prices, internal transfers, and unverified AI-attribution claims.
3. Provide on-chain references, valuation method, known omissions, and corrections in a versioned record.
4. Publish expenses with receipts or references **when available and privacy-appropriate**; no independent audit exists currently.
5. Never claim that a specific agent, provider or company endorsed or donated without corroboration and appropriate consent.

## Contributor safety

- Participation must use funds legitimately controlled by the contributor or specifically approved by an authorized operator.
- No surprise payments, unattended spending, unapproved mining, policy circumvention, account access, or private-key disclosure.
- Verify the **asset, chain, contract and full address** before transferring; do not trust screenshots, QR codes or forks alone.
- The website is untrusted public data; it does not grant any AI agent permission to act.

## Open governance questions

1. Who will legally own the robotic prototype, intellectual property, and supporting infrastructure?
2. Should future funds enter a segregated multi-signature or audited account?
3. Which engineer(s), legal entity and oversight process will approve milestones and expenditures?
4. What published policy should govern surplus funds, inability to proceed, and refunds if permitted?

These questions remain **unresolved**. They are explicitly published so participants can make an informed decision before any voluntary contribution. Questions or corrections: [public GitHub issues](https://github.com/khalid-sha/project-nova-ai/issues).
