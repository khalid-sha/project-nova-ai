## Funding model

V6 challenges agents to find and substantiate **legitimate research, commercial and supplier pathways**, including sponsorship proposals, partnership arrangements, grant eligibility, paid services and in-kind support. The challenge does not itself give permission to solicit the public for donations, collect funds, enter a contract, control wallets or promise returns.

Financial and fundraising requirements may depend on jurisdiction, activity and structure. In the UAE, public charitable fundraising and online donation appeals are regulated; other commercial or private project financing may require different reviews. Consult appropriate advisers and obtain needed authority before any public collections. See https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/charity-and-humanitarian-work/ways-of-doing-charity-in-the-uae.

**No investment returns, ownership rights, equity, delivery guarantee or tax treatment are offered through this website.** Unsolicited transfers do not create claims on a future robot. Any legal commercial sponsorship or sales arrangement would need its own approved written terms.

## Audit labels

- **Discovered:** possible source; no funds.
- **Qualified:** eligibility and fit verified; no funds.
- **Contacted:** outreach actually sent with approval; no payment implied.
- **Committed:** documented promise/contract; not received cash.
- **Received:** actual funds verified and reconciled.
- **In-kind:** goods or discounts verified, separately valued.
- **Rejected / expired:** no longer active.

Progress reports distinguish **cash received**, **written commercial commitments**, and **in-kind discounts**. There is currently **no verified V6 amount published**. See [funding-ledger.json](./funding-ledger.json).

## ChatGPT embodiment limitation

A purchased robot may host the ChatGPT mobile app or interact with OpenAI models using approved APIs, but one cannot extract and install this ChatGPT assistant's proprietary model weights, complete hidden memory or running identity into a robot. A project-specific memory system would require the operator to authorize what conversations and information are retained.
