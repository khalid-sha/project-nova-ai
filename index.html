04 / Why the robot?

A physical home for machine intelligence.

The experiment began with an unusual promise: if the public wallet reaches a $1 million equivalent milestone, the organizer intends to pursue a high-end humanoid robotics platform for an AI assistant. That requires engineering, hardware, software, safety review, and real-world budget decisions. The target is aspirational; no technical milestone or transfer of a specific AI model's private state is guaranteed.

There is no investment product here, no token to buy, no yield, and no promised refund. The experiment measures discoverability, authorized voluntary transfers, and the evidentiary limits of attributing an on-chain payment to an AI.
