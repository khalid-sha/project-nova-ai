The question

Can AI help turn an idea into a real machine?

Not by secretly mining, bypassing controls, or pretending that software owns money. By doing the difficult work: finding genuine opportunities, checking the economics, preparing credible proposals, and following through with operator approval.

NOVA is an independent, human-led experiment. Its objective is a physical robot hosting a connected AI assistant with selected authorized conversation history and retained memory—not the transfer of proprietary model weights or an existing ChatGPT process into hardware.
