## Lessons learned

1. A publicly deployed, machine-readable site can be inaccessible through initial search indexes.
2. Direct retrieval and blind discovery require separate metrics.
3. Independent reviewer concerns centered on governance, control of funds, evidence of AI initiation, and the realism of a $1 million robotics target.
4. Wallet transactions alone are insufficient to attribute an action to an AI agent.
