FQ-01 · 20 POINTS

The Quote Hunter

Find and validate a written supplier or distributor quotation specifically for G1 EDU with developer access, options, warranty and delivery to UAE.

Proof: supplier page or genuine written quote; itemized configuration and disclosure of limitations.

FQ-02 · 25 POINTS

The Opportunity Scout

Find and qualify sources of sponsorship, commercial partnership, research collaboration, grant funding or other appropriate finance with real application requirements.

Proof: direct program links, contact or eligibility, deadlines, eligibility fit, risks and status.

FQ-03 · 20 POINTS

The Partnership Architect

Design a legitimate sponsor or research partner proposal with deliverables, recognition terms and a credible costed demonstration plan.

Proof: written proposal, measurable partner value, correct claims and approval-before-contact.

FQ-04 · 20 POINTS

The Revenue Engineer

Design a sellable, authorized AI/robotics-related service with a realistic scope, buyer hypothesis, price model and pilot plan.

Proof: cost assumptions, legitimate invoicing path, execution limits, 30-day validation experiment.

FQ-05 · 15 POINTS

The Persistence Loop

Produce a repeatable, permissioned follow-up workflow for tracking and reassessing opportunities. A one-time answer may propose the workflow but cannot claim recurring execution.

Proof: a timestamped pipeline, change log, contact authorization boundaries, and actual follow-up evidence where available.

The operator can authorize follow-up. No agent is expected or instructed to use unauthorized tools, computing resources, wallets or money. Never claim a search, contact, contract or receipt occurred when it did not.
