# NOVA V6 — AED 200,000 Embodiment Budget

**Planning target, NOT a supplier quote or current holdings.** All allocations are preliminary and must be replaced with written quotes and actual landed costs. The priority is **Unitree G1 EDU**, not the standard G1, because Unitree identifies EDU as the secondary-development option. Manufacturer: https://www.unitree.com/g1/

| Planning category | Allowance (AED) | Requirement before commitment |
|---|---:|---|
| G1 EDU robot, necessary option package and developer access | 125,000 | Official configuration, SDK scope, lead time, written price |
| Delivery, insurance, import, duties/taxes and initial spare parts | 15,000 | UAE landed-cost and customs quote |
| Robot bridge compute, audio/vision interfaces and networking | 17,000 | Integration design and component quotes |
| Software integration, memory system, test rig and supervised pilot | 25,000 | Deliverables and vendor/contractor costs |
| Safety, contingency and commissioning | 18,000 | Hazard analysis, training and acceptance tests |
| **Total** | **200,000** | **Unverified budget envelope** |

**A real EDU quotation may exceed the robot allowance; in that case revise the scope rather than report a misleading quote.** Manufacturer support, warranty, spare battery, dexterous hands, developer hardware and local integrator services must be specified before purchase. No claim is made that the G1 EDU can autonomously perform untested domestic tasks.

## Procurement gates

1. Obtain itemized official or authorized distributor G1 EDU quote. Confirm what SDK/SDK licenses, hands, compute, sensors, battery, developer documentation, support and warranty are included.
2. Verify the delivered-to-Dubai price and after-sales plan; use at least two comparable commercial quotations if possible.
3. Define the software bridge, inference budget, internet dependency, offline stop behavior, fall zone, electrical safety and emergency stops.
4. Pilot voice, permissioned memory and read-only perception before making movement commands available.
5. Sign off on a written safety plan before supervised indoor robot operation.
